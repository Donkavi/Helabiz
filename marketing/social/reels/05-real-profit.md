## 7. Format and technical requirements

- 1080×1920 at 30 fps, loopable, at the length given in the brief.
- Render everything onto **one `<canvas>`**, scaled to fit the window at 9:16, so it can be recorded.
- Drive all animation from one master timeline measured in seconds, with custom easing (easeOutBack, easeInOutCubic, spring overshoot). Structure the code by scene, with each scene a function of its local time.
- **Beat grid.** One beat = 0.5s. Write a beat-grid helper and land every cut and text slam on a beat.
- **Reels safe zones:** keep important text and the logo out of the top 220px, the bottom 400px and the right 140px.
- **Controls** go outside the canvas and are hidden while recording: ▶ Play (audio needs a user gesture), Replay, a scrubber labelled with the scene names, a mute toggle and "⬇ Export video".
- **Export.**
  - Combine `canvas.captureStream(30)` with the Web Audio output through a `MediaStreamAudioDestinationNode`.
  - Record with `MediaRecorder` at about 12 Mbps. Prefer `video/mp4` if `MediaRecorder.isTypeSupported` reports it, otherwise use `video/webm;codecs=vp9,opus`.
  - Download the file automatically when the timeline ends.
- **Sound.** Everything is synthesized with the Web Audio API; no audio files. Mix the sound effects under the music, duck the music by about 4dB on big hits, and put a limiter (DynamicsCompressor) on the master bus.
- **No external images.** Draw every illustration and icon as canvas vector shapes. Don't rely on emoji for important visuals. Mockups of other apps (chat apps, social pages) must be generic, with no real brand logos.

## 8. Motion-design quality bar

- **Easing:** nothing moves linearly. Use easing, overshoot, anticipation and follow-through, with a stagger of 30–60ms between siblings.
- **Transitions:** mix the styles: whip pans with drawn motion trails, jade mask wipes, zoom-throughs and match cuts on shapes and colours.
- **Kinetic type:** words land on the beat. Highlight key words with a jade underline or a pill shape behind them.
- **Constant subtle life:** drifting background shapes or film grain, gentle parallax and a soft "breathing" camera scale.
- **Readability first:** short lines stay on screen for at least 1.2s. Keep contrast high and never put text over busy areas.
- **Performance:** keep a smooth 30fps. Precompute what you can and don't allocate new objects every frame.
