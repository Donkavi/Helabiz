# Helabiz reel series

Eight short reels, each built from its own prompt in Claude Desktop. Reel 1 is the launch reel
(`../reel-prompt.md`). Reels 2–8 are in this folder.

One reel can't win customers on its own. People usually need to see a brand several times, from
different angles, before they act. Each reel answers a different reason someone hesitates:

Every reel ends on the same end card and the same three-note sound, so by the third reel people
recognise Helabiz before they read the name.

## How to make each reel

1. Open a **new chat** in Claude Desktop for each reel. Don't continue in the old one.
2. Attach the three logo files from `marketing/social/logo/`: `lockup-dark.png`, `lockup-light.png` and `mark-transparent-1024.png`.
3. Paste the whole prompt file.
4. When it's built, press ▶ Play to check it, then "⬇ Export video".
   - If the file comes out as `.webm`, convert it to `.mp4` before uploading. Any free online converter or CapCut works.
5. Optional: record the Sinhala voice-over Claude writes and add it in CapCut.

## Posting plan (about three weeks)

Post around **7–9pm**, when Sri Lankan feeds are busiest. Post each reel to Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

| Week | Day | Reel | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon | 1 Launch | Introduces Helabiz. Pin it to the top. |
| 1 | Wed | 2 POV Inbox | Funny and relatable, so it's the most likely to be shared. |
| 1 | Sat | 3 5-minute challenge | Asks for comments, which boosts reach. |
| 2 | Tue | 4 Lakhs vs රු. 0 | Answers the price question once people are curious. |
| 2 | Thu | 7 Templates | Asks for comments. Every niche sees itself. |
| 2 | Sat | 5 Real profit | Shows it's more than a website. |
| 3 | Tue | 6 We build it | Catches people who were interested but scared. |
| 3 | Fri | 8 Order journey | The strongest product demo, saved for people who already know Helabiz. |

**After 48 hours,** see which reel kept people watching longest (Insights → average watch time) and
boost that one with a small paid budget. The best performer is usually the one with the strongest
first two seconds, so reuse that hook style in later reels.

**Reply to every comment** on reels 3, 5 and 7, which all ask people to comment. Replies count as
engagement and push the reel to more people.
